# Personal Engineering Knowledge Base

> Deep technical notes from years of certifications and real-world software engineering. A living reference — not just a list of badges.

## What's in here

This knowledge base documents everything I have learned across my engineering career — from certification objectives to practical patterns I use daily on the job.

Every topic follows a consistent structure:

- **Overview** — what it is and why it exists
- **Core Concepts** — the fundamentals you must know
- **Practical Examples** — real code and configurations
- **Best Practices** — production-ready patterns
- **Common Mistakes** — what to avoid
- **Interview Questions** — key questions with clear answers
- **Cheat Sheet** — the most important facts at a glance
- **Certification Coverage** — how this maps to exam objectives
- **My Preparation Strategy** — how I personally studied and passed
