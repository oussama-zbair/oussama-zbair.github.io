## What Is This Knowledge Base?

This is my **personal engineering handbook**.

It was created to organize and preserve the technical knowledge I acquire throughout my career — instead of letting it disappear after completing a certification, finishing a course, or ending a project.

Unlike traditional study notes, this documentation is designed to **evolve continuously** as I gain new experience and discover better practices. Every page represents knowledge that I consider valuable enough to remember, revisit, and apply in real software engineering work.

:::tip Philosophy
The goal is not to collect certificates — it is to build a **reusable knowledge system** that compounds over time.
:::

## How to Use This Documentation

<Tabs>
  <TabItem value="interview" label="🎯 Interview Prep" default>

**Goal:** Quickly review a topic before a technical interview.

1. Open the relevant topic from the sidebar
2. Skip directly to the **Interview Questions** section at the bottom
3. Read the **Cheat Sheet** for key facts and commands
4. Done — you're ready in under 5 minutes

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="learning" label="📚 Learning a Topic">

**Goal:** Understand something new from scratch.

1. Start with the **Overview** — understand why this technology exists
2. Read **Core Concepts** to build a mental model
3. Study the **Practical Examples** with real code
4. Apply it in a small personal project

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="reference" label="🔍 Quick Reference">

**Goal:** Find a specific command, definition, or pattern fast.

1. Use the **search bar** (top right) with any keyword
2. Jump directly to the **Cheat Sheet** section of the relevant topic
3. Use the **Table of Contents** on the right side of each article

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="deep" label="🎓 Deep Study">

**Goal:** Master a topic completely.

1. Read the **complete article** from top to bottom
2. Explore the **official references** linked at the bottom
3. Do the **hands-on practice** exercises
4. Write your own **personal notes** in the designated section

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
